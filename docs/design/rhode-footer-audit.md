# Rhode Footer Audit

Date: 2026-06-20

Reference: https://www.rhodeskin.com/

This audit records structural observations only. Do not copy Rhode wording, code,
logos, legal text, social links, payment marks, or policy language into
Mei-Pelle.

## Structural Observations

- Footer is inset from the viewport rather than flush edge-to-edge.
- Background is a warm light neutral; text is low-contrast grey-brown.
- First region is an oversized brand/logo zone occupying roughly half the
  footer height on large desktop.
- Content region pairs newsletter/signup copy with navigation columns.
- Desktop columns include Navigate, Social, Official, and Support.
- Newsletter field and submit button sit in one horizontal row on desktop and
  remain prominent on mobile.
- Legal/privacy controls appear in the support/bottom area.
- Locale selector is present with many country/region options.
- Payment-provider marks appear because Rhode has real commerce.
- Mobile stacks the regions; navigation remains visible rather than becoming a
  tiny horizontal legal row.
- Footer ends long and short pages with a strong editorial block instead of a
  generic sitemap strip.

## Mei-Pelle Adaptation

- Use a large MEI-PELLE wordmark and a substantial brand/updates region.
- Keep Navigate, Support, and Official groups.
- Omit Social until real approved profiles exist.
- Omit payment marks while checkout is disabled.
- Omit locale/country selector until region commerce exists.
- Use Cookie Preferences and Your Privacy Choices as real controls/routes.
- Preserve generous gutters and a tall closing rhythm without copying Rhode's
  exact typography, wording, logo, or CSS.
