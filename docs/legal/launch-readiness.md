# Legal and Launch Readiness Open Items

Date: 2026-06-20

These facts are unresolved and should be confirmed by the owner and, where
appropriate, counsel before public launch.

- Legal entity name.
- Business mailing address.
- Privacy contact channel.
- Support contact channel and response expectations.
- Governing law.
- Dispute-resolution approach.
- Product-sale terms.
- Checkout/payment provider.
- Shipping regions.
- Tax calculation.
- Return/refund policy.
- Warranty policy.
- Order support workflow.
- Marketing-email provider and unsubscribe workflow.
- Contact-message transport and retention.
- Analytics providers, if any.
- Advertising/pixel providers, if any.
- International data-transfer arrangements.
- California and other U.S. state privacy threshold applicability.
- EEA/UK representative requirements, if applicable.
- Provider log retention windows.
- Account deletion and data-export workflow.
- Final effective dates for Privacy, Terms, Cookie Policy, and Accessibility.
- Accessibility audit or conformance report scope, if one is commissioned.
