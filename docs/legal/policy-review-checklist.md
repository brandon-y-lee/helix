# Legal Policy Review Checklist

Last updated: June 24, 2026

This checklist is for owner and counsel review before Mei Pelle treats the
support and legal pages as launch-ready.

## Before Public Launch

- Confirm the legal business entity, mailing address, jurisdiction, governing law, and dispute terms.
- Publish a verified public support destination.
- Publish a verified privacy request intake channel.
- Confirm whether a phone number, email address, physical address, registered agent, or privacy agent must appear in any policy.
- Review Privacy Policy coverage for Supabase, Algolia, Vercel/Next.js, Google Fonts, cookies, guest cart tokens, account data, search queries, and logs.
- Review whether state privacy laws require additional notices, request verification flows, or opt-out controls.
- Confirm child-directed-content positioning and age restrictions.
- Confirm accessibility statement scope, WCAG target, feedback route, and any known limitation disclosures.
- Confirm all policy dates, canonical URLs, sitemap entries, and footer links.

## Before Checkout or Orders

- Add payment processor terms, payment-method disclosures, fraud review, tax handling, shipping address collection, billing address collection, order confirmation, cancellation, and chargeback language.
- Add final shipping prices, shipping destinations, carrier responsibilities, delivery estimates, tracking rules, lost-package handling, refused-package handling, PO box and freight-forwarder rules, and holiday/sale delay rules.
- Add final return/refund/exchange procedures, required authorization, condition rules, exclusions, return shipping responsibility, refund timing, and damaged/missing/incorrect item process.
- Confirm whether free standard shipping applies before or after discounts, before or after tax, and whether it excludes gift cards, samples, subscriptions, or final-sale items.
- Verify that cart, checkout, order email, and account-order-history behavior match the published terms.

## Before Marketing, Analytics, or Advertising

- Add newsletter signup data flow, consent language, unsubscribe process, provider list, and retention rules.
- Add analytics and advertising providers, cookie categories, consent controls, and any sale/share or targeted advertising disclosures.
- Confirm whether email, SMS, loyalty, referrals, affiliate, or subscription programs need separate terms.

## Before Rewards, Loyalty, or Referral Programs

- Publish eligibility, earning, redemption, expiration, abuse, cancellation, return, transfer, and modification rules.
- Confirm how discounts interact with shipping thresholds, taxes, returns, exchanges, and promotional exclusions.
- Verify rewards balances and redemptions against implemented account and checkout systems.

## Evidence to Archive

- Final policy text approved for launch.
- Provider data-processing agreements and retention settings.
- Screenshot or PDF archive of published policies.
- Accessibility testing results and known issues.
- Sign-off for support intake, privacy intake, checkout, shipping, returns, and rewards readiness.
