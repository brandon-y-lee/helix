## Scope and evidence rules

This review uses only current first-party Leaders USA/Korea pages and the already-published [Leaders Formula and Catalog Evidence](./leaders-catalog-evidence.md). Online status was checked on 2026-08-09. Leaders' status signals are internally inconsistent: US collection filters report products in stock while individual page markup can contain both “Sold out” and “Add to cart,” and the Korean PHA page is sold out. Retail status is therefore non-decisive and does not prove OEM availability.

An ingredient is treated as present only when an official ingredient declaration or official package image names it. Product names such as “peeling,” “pore,” and “blemish” are not ingredient evidence. “Complete INCI” below means the official page publishes a conventional ingredient list; it is still not a substitute for a signed specification for the exact OEM formula.

Offer four shopper-legible REFINE choices, each as a **separate Product** connected through a REFINE family selector:

This set gives customers four differences they can understand before reading ingredients: **weekly exfoliation, daily pore/oil balance, post-blemish tone care, and cooling comfort**. It avoids padding the selector with several hydration-first formulas that differ mostly in ingredient-story copy.

## Evidence for the recommended set

### 1. PEEL STEP PHA Deep Peeling Pad — acid/exfoliating

- **Listing and format:** Leaders USA currently lists the three-product PEEL STEP line; the collection filter reports it in stock while Product markup is ambiguous. Korea lists the PHA SKU as sold out. The Product is 215 mL / 70 pads with a water-light essence and dual-texture pad. [Official PEEL STEP collection](https://www.leaderscosmeticsusa.com/collections/peel-step-line) · [Official PHA Product](https://www.leaderscosmeticsusa.com/products/peel-step-pha-deep-peeling-pad)
- **Complete official declaration:** the [current Korean Product](https://m.leaderscosmetics.com/product/%ED%95%84%EC%8A%A4%ED%85%9D-%ED%8C%8C%ED%95%98-%EB%94%A5-%ED%95%84%EB%A7%81-%ED%8C%A8%EB%93%9C-215ml-70%EB%A7%A4/1257/) lists Water, Methylpropanediol, Niacinamide, Butylene Glycol, 1,2-Hexanediol, Trehalose, Glycerin, Allantoin, Ethylhexylglycerin, Caprylyl Glycol, Papaya Fruit Extract, Sodium Hyaluronate, Dipropylene Glycol, Disodium EDTA, Sodium Citrate, **Gluconolactone (100 ppm / 0.01%)**, plum, apple and grape extracts, and **Capryloyl Salicylic Acid (LHA)**.
- **Acid evidence:** both a PHA and LHA are therefore present in the exact retail declaration rather than inferred from “peeling.” The disclosed gluconolactone level is low, and the LHA concentration and finished pH are not published; presence alone does not establish exfoliating potency.
- **Cadence:** Leaders directs 1–3 uses weekly, followed by moisturizer and daytime SPF; it recommends a simple, hydrating routine on exfoliation days.
- **Claim posture:** current copy confines benefits to the appearance of pores, rough texture, dullness, and radiance. It makes no acne-treatment claim.
- **Hard limitation:** the USA page omits the INCI while Korea publishes one, so market/formula equivalence must be confirmed. LHA concentration, finished pH, acid availability, tolerance, and exact OEM equivalence remain unknown. This is a **conditional selection**, not a launch-cleared formula.
- **Portfolio role:** this is the only currently listed Leaders pad with direct first-party evidence of named exfoliating acids. It is the best conditional replacement for unsupported “active pad” language on the current non-acid formula.

### 2. Pore Tightening Pad — pore/oil balance, not acid exfoliation

- **Listing and format:** the official toner-pad collection lists all four products and reports them in stock, although individual Product markup is ambiguous. The Pore pad is 150 mL / 50 pads and uses an embossed side for residue/oil removal plus a smooth side for a 3–5 minute targeted pad mask. Leaders directs daily use. [Official toner-pad collection](https://www.leaderscosmeticsusa.com/collections/leaders-toner-pads) · [Official Pore Product](https://www.leaderscosmeticsusa.com/products/leaders-pore-tightening-toner-pads-50-pads-170-ml)
- **Complete official declaration:** Water, Betaine, 1,2-Hexanediol, Panthenol, Butylene Glycol, Ethylhexylglycerin, Caprylyl Glycol, Adenosine, Disodium EDTA, Sodium Hyaluronate, plum fruit extract, seaweed extracts, Glycerin, Peppermint Extract, Collagen, and Palmitoyl Tripeptide-5.
- **Acid check:** no AHA, BHA, PHA, or other named exfoliating acid appears. The embossed pad can provide wiping/physical refinement, but this does not make the essence a chemical exfoliant.
- **Claim posture:** the official page uses cosmetic appearance language—refined-looking pores, smoother texture, oil balance, and hydration. Use **pore appearance**, never literal pore shrinking or tightening.
- **Reconciliation:** the official URL retains “170 ml,” while current page content says 150 mL; the supplier deck reported 170 mL. Confirm the OEM fill before final Product Facts.

### 3. Vita Blemish Pad — brightening/post-blemish appearance

- **Current status and format:** 120 mL / 80 soft, essence-saturated pads with a fresh, light, non-sticky finish; daily swipe or 3–5 minute targeted placement. [Official Vita Product](https://www.leaderscosmeticsusa.com/products/leaders-vita-blemish-pad-80-pads-x-120ml)
- **Complete official declaration:** Water, Glycerin, **Niacinamide**, 1,2-Hexanediol, Butylene Glycol, Polyglyceryl-10 Laurate, Caprylyl Glycol, Ethylhexylglycerin, Buteth-3, Disodium EDTA, Xanthan Gum, Sodium Benzotriazolyl Butylphenol Sulfonate, Alcohol, Gardenia Flower Extract, Tris(Tetramethylhydroxypiperidinol) Citrate, Tributyl Citrate, a sophorolipid ferment, Dipropylene Glycol, Glutathione, Ascorbic Acid, and Sunflower Seed Oil.
- **Concentrations:** none are disclosed. Niacinamide is third in the official declaration, but its percentage must not be invented.
- **Acid check:** no AHA, BHA, or PHA appears. Ascorbic Acid is present near the end of the declaration as an antioxidant/brightening ingredient, not evidence of an exfoliating-acid treatment.
- **Claim posture:** Leaders positions it for the **appearance** of dark spots, post-blemish marks, uneven tone, dullness, and rough texture. “Blemish” here does not establish an acne-drug formula. Mei Pelle should not claim that it treats, prevents, or clears acne.

### 4. TECA Cooling Pad — soothing/cooling

- **Current status and format:** 130 mL / 80 soft, essence-saturated pads with a light cooling finish; daily swipe or 3–5 minute targeted placement. [Official TECA Product](https://www.leaderscosmeticsusa.com/products/leaders-teca-cooling-pad-80-pads-x-130ml)
- **Complete official declaration:** a humectant base followed by Allantoin, Centella Leaf Extract, botanical oils/extracts, Peppermint Extract, Guaiazulene, Lactobacillus/Lactococcus/Bifida ferment lysates, Ethanol, Panthenol, Centella triterpenes, Ceramide NP, and Beta-Glucan. The page discloses no active percentage.
- **Acid check:** no AHA, BHA, or PHA appears.
- **Claim posture:** Leaders uses cooling, comfort, hydration, and improved appearance of redness. Mei Pelle should retain “feels cool/comforted” and “calmer-looking” language, avoiding inflammation-treatment claims.
- **Formula-fit caution:** peppermint extract and ethanol are present. “Sensitive skin” and redness language requires finished-formula tolerance evidence; ingredient storytelling alone is insufficient.

## Product and PDP architecture

The four recommendations must not become variants of one Product. They differ in Formula, ingredient declaration, cadence, quantity/fill, intended skin state, directions, and safety messaging. A variant model would incorrectly imply that only a selectable attribute changes while Product Facts remain shared.

Use one REFINE family experience that links four canonical Product records:

```text
REFINE
What does your skin need?

Exfoliate weekly  → PHA + LHA exfoliating pads
Balance pores     → Daily pore toner pads
Brighten marks    → Brightening treatment pads
Cool and calm     → Cooling treatment pads
```

The selector may appear on every related PDP, but selecting an option should navigate to or load the selected Product's own canonical URL, Formula facts, directions, price, availability, imagery, and claims. Cart lines and inventory must retain the exact Product/Variant identity.

## Supplier questions and launch gates

1. Confirm that all four exact retail formulas are available for Mei Pelle OEM production and provide signed formula/SKU mappings.
2. Supply signed Complete INCI, quantitative formula, pH, specifications, stability, preservative/challenge, compatibility, irritation/sensitization, and packaging reports for each exact formula; reconcile the USA and Korean formula versions.
3. For PHA Deep, confirm the published 100 ppm gluconolactone, capryloyl salicylic acid percentage, total acid system, finished pH, neutralization, intended exposure, and evidence supporting meaningful exfoliation and the 1–3-times-weekly cadence.
4. Confirm whether the PHA, Cica, and Panthenol PEEL STEP formulas are absent from the supplier deck because they are newer, market-specific, or unavailable for OEM production; separately confirm whether the legacy salicylic-acid Teatree formula remains manufacturable.
5. Reconcile Pore Tightening Pad at 150 versus 170 mL and confirm whether Mei Pelle's current Complete INCI matches the intended production formula.
6. Provide finished-formula substantiation for pore appearance/oil balance, post-blemish tone, cooling/redness appearance, sensitive-skin suitability, dermatologist-tested, non-comedogenic, and exfoliation claims.
7. Confirm fragrance, essential-oil, allergen, animal-derived, and vegan status from signed specifications rather than retail summaries.
8. Conduct system-wear and tolerability testing with Biotic Reset, Peptide Bounce, Ceramide Cushion, shaving, and daytime sunscreen. Exfoliation-day education should prevent unnecessary stacking if irritation emerges.

## Decision conclusion

The source gap is now narrow: Leaders has one clearly acid-containing pad, **PEEL STEP PHA Deep Peeling Pad**, but 100 ppm gluconolactone plus undisclosed LHA and pH do not establish potency. Select it conditionally as REFINE's weekly active option only after Leaders substantiates the finished formula. Retain the current Pore formula as an explicitly non-acid daily option, and add Vita and TECA for clearly different brightening and cooling needs. Keep PDRN and nutrition pads outside REFINE; the exact Korean declarations confirm that the overlapping Cica and Panthenol “peeling” pads contain no AHA, BHA, or PHA.
