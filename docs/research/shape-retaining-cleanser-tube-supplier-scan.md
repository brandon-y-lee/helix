# White Shape-Retaining Cleanser-Tube Supplier Scan

- **Research ticket:** [#259 — Find white shape-retaining cleanser-tube suppliers](https://github.com/brandon-y-lee/helix/issues/259)
- **Research date:** 2026-08-31
- **Decision served:** identify credible manufacturers for a white cleanser tube that substantially recovers its profile after dispensing, in 30 mL Mini and 120 mL Full Size, while preserving 40, 100, and 150 mL alternatives.
- **Launch planning quantity:** 1,000–3,000 primary containers per Product, subject to component-specific order increments and decoration minimums.
- **Evidence boundary:** public first-party manufacturer material only; no supplier outreach, private quotations, samples, audits, or factory verification were authorized for this ticket.

> **Screening report, not an award or purchase specification.** “Shape-retaining” is a measured package behavior, not a resin name. A supplier's material claim, photograph, “soft touch” description, or use of the word “recovery” does not prove performance with the helix Formula. No public evidence reviewed establishes a supplier for Rhode Pineapple Refresh, and visual similarity is not supplier evidence. Final selection requires the common RFQ in #263, production-tool samples, complete component declarations, Formula compatibility and recovery tests, quality qualification, and comparable written offers.

## Executive decision

The public record supports a competitive RFQ, but **does not support naming the cheapest qualified supplier**. None of the candidates publishes a current, configuration-complete price for the same white 30/120 mL tube pair delivered to the same fill route. The only public dollar anchor located is a legacy Montebello Tube Store workbook listing selected stock tubes at US$1.00 each; its listed laminate sizes do not include the target 30 or 120 mL formats and it is not a current comparable offer. Under the [landed-cost and evidence model](./packaging-landed-cost-evidence-model.md), an indicative ranking needs E3 offers with a common drawing, bill of materials, quantity split, decoration, Incoterms rule and named place, quality scope, and route to Southern California.

The most defensible sourcing strategy is a three-architecture competition:

1. **Resilient coextruded PE/EVOH or plastic-barrier laminate.** This is the closest technical route to a soft, squeezeable tube that substantially rebounds. Joopoong explicitly claims “excellent recovery” for its mLLDPE/EVOH tube; Montebello says its plastic-barrier laminate has “bounce back memory” and retains its shape; EPL says its all-polyethylene Green Maple Leaf laminate retains shape after repeated dispensing. These are supplier claims to test, not accepted performance. [Joopoong](https://joopoong.co.kr/en/) · [Montebello PBL](https://www.montebellopkg.com/laminate-tubes/) · [EPL Green Maple Leaf](https://www.eplglobal.com/product/green-maple-leaf/)
2. **Injection-molded polypropylene with in-mold label.** ICONS America, Viva, and CTLpack offer molded tube systems whose body, label, shoulder, and closure can be configured as predominantly PP. This route offers highly controlled geometry and broad decoration, but may feel firmer and evacuate differently from an extruded tube. Public construction data do not prove the requested rebound, so it stays in the competition only through the same measured protocol. [ICONS IML tubes](https://icons-america.com/portfolio/in-mold-label-tubes/) · [Viva round tubes](https://viva-healthcare.com/en/round-tube.php) · [CTLpack](https://www.ctlpack.com/?lang=en)
3. **Single-, two-, or multilayer PE as a cost/control challenger.** Daiwa, Takemoto, CCL Tube, Pumtech/Bookuk, ATEC, THE IM, SR Packaging, Wanhong, and Paragon can supply or develop PE tubes. Barrier needs must be derived from the Formula and shelf-life protocol; a cheaper single-layer construction is not a benefit if it fails mass-loss, odor, ingredient, paneling, stress-cracking, or decoration tests.

**Recommended first RFQ/sample wave after #263:** Joopoong, ICONS America, Montebello, CCL Tube, SR Packaging, Viva, Daiwa Can, Takemoto Packaging, EPL, ALLTUB, and CTLpack. This is deliberately broader than an award shortlist. It balances supplier-claimed recovery, exact size coverage, launch-scale hypotheses, domestic logistics, and technical comparators. Neopac remains a strong technical benchmark but its current public minimums appear above the 1,000–3,000 launch objective. Sanying's public 30,000-unit MOQ is also a commercial mismatch. Toly is a screened lead, not a qualified candidate, because its public tube page does not identify the actual conventional-tube factory.

**Recommended nominal pair remains 30 mL / 1.01 U.S. fl oz Mini and 120 mL / 4.06 U.S. fl oz Full Size.** The pair best fits the trial and 60–90-day design cases in the [cleanser benchmark](./cleanser-packaging-size-supply-chain-benchmark.md). Retain 40 mL / 1.35 fl oz and 100 or 150 mL / 3.38 or 5.07 fl oz as quoted alternates until dose, residual, dimensions, filling, and landed-cost evidence are available.

## Scope, definitions, and evidence rules

### What the requested behavior means

For this report, a **shape-retaining** tube is a squeezeable primary container that, after a controlled dispense, substantially returns toward its pre-dispense sidewall profile rather than recording a permanent fold. It is not required to look perfectly new after every use. The useful measures are residual deformation, recovery time, force to dispense, evacuation, and behavior over the package life.

This brief is distinct from the fold-retaining, paint-tube-like Aesop reference. A multilayer or aluminum-barrier tube may remain collapsed by design. That can be aesthetically attractive, but it is not a substitute for resilient recovery. The [cleanser benchmark](./cleanser-packaging-size-supply-chain-benchmark.md) preserves both experiences as separate technical tracks and cautions that the Aesop reference does not establish aluminum, gauge, or supplier.

The Rhode image is used only to communicate a **behavioral and visual starting point**: white, clean, flexible, and not visibly crumpled after normal use. It does not establish the package's resin, laminate, gauge, converter, filler, or source. The helix design should also maintain distance from the reference composite by using a visible shoulder-to-cap step, a materially different cap ratio or profile, and independent artwork rather than reproducing the flush oversized cap, shoulder continuity, cream-on-cream palette, and vertical wordmark combination identified in the [design-rights screen](./reference-packaging-design-rights-screen.md).

### Evidence vocabulary

| Term | Meaning in this report |
| --- | --- |
| **Verified public capability** | A manufacturer-owned page, catalog, certificate, or document supports the stated capability. It may be a platform-level fact rather than an exact SKU fact. |
| **Supplier performance claim** | The manufacturer describes recovery, barrier, sustainability, quality, or lead-time performance. It remains unverified for the helix configuration. |
| **Supported fit** | Published diameter/capacity data encompass a target size, or a published range strongly supports it. Exact drawing and brimful/fill conventions still require confirmation. |
| **Conditional candidate** | The manufacturer and relevant capability are credible, but one or more launch-critical fields are not public. |
| **Unknown** | Public first-party evidence reviewed does not establish the field. Unknown does not mean absent and is never entered as zero cost or zero risk. |

Public pages were reviewed as of the research date. Supplier pages and catalogs can change. Certification claims require the exact legal entity, manufacturing site, scope, standard, certificate number, issuer, and current validity at qualification.

## Detailed supplier evidence

### South Korea

#### 1. Joopoong — strongest public recovery claim

- **Identity and factory.** Joopoong identifies itself as a tube manufacturer with an Anseong, South Korea factory and shows an integrated sequence spanning material mixing, extrusion, head injection, printing, stamping, cap injection, assembly, sealing, inspection and packing. That is materially stronger factory disclosure than a marketer showing only finished packs. [Joopoong](https://joopoong.co.kr/en/)
- **Construction, barrier, gauge and recovery.** Its facial-care proposition identifies mLLDPE and a five-layer structure with EVOH, describes soft touch, and expressly claims “excellent recovery.” The page does not publish resin grades, individual layer thicknesses, total gauge, oxygen/water-vapor transmission values, recovery percentage, recovery time, or test conditions. Treat recovery as a supplier claim requiring the helix protocol.
- **Capacity, neck, closure and seal.** The reviewed public page does not provide a dimensional capacity table. Joopoong shows PP one-touch, screw and gasket cap capability plus mold making, but not exact neck finish, orifice, torque, membrane, liner, induction-seal, or target-size fit. Request 30/40 and 100/120/150 mL drawings with at least two orifices.
- **White finish and decoration.** The manufacturer publishes silk screen, offset and hot-stamp capability and gloss/matte options. Opaque white resin/masterbatch, cap color match, ΔE, gloss, ink system, seam and rub resistance are unknown.
- **Stock/custom, MOQ, tooling, price and lead time.** Public numeric MOQ, stock-platform status, order increment, tooling ownership/cost/life, sample time, production lead, price, payment and Incoterms terms are absent. No cost rank is possible.
- **Quality, sustainability, logistics and risk.** Joopoong claims ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001 and an SGS audit; certificate entity, site, scope, number and current validity must be checked. Component weights, PCR/bio-based options, recyclability assessment, toxics certificates and packout are not public. **Disposition: first-wave recovery specialist, conditional on exact-size drawings and launch MOQ.**

#### 2. Pumtech / Bookuk T&C — established Korean tube group, factory nomination required

- **Identity and capability.** Pumtech identifies Bookuk T&C as a group company founded in 1969 and describes it as Korea's first pharmaceutical and cosmetic tube company. Bookuk's public group profile lists aluminum, PE, Polyfoil and laminate tubes. Pumtech also identifies tube assembly, printing, stamping and injection capability in its factory network. [Pumtech group](https://www.pumtech.com/eng/s1/s1_1.php) · [Pumtech facilities](https://www.pumtech.com/eng/s1/s1_2.php)
- **Construction, barrier, gauge and recovery.** The public categories establish multiple tube architectures but do not provide layer order, resins, barrier metrics, gauge, or rebound behavior for a proposed cleanser tube. Polyfoil is a trademark/platform term elsewhere in the market; the exact Bookuk construction must be declared rather than inferred.
- **Format and aesthetics.** Exact capacity, diameter, neck, orifice, seal, closure, white finish, decoration method and cap tooling for 30/120 mL are not public on the reviewed corporate pages.
- **Commercial and operational evidence.** Numeric component MOQ, stock/custom status, tooling, price, lead time and packout are unknown. The RFQ must name Bookuk's producing legal entity and site rather than accepting a group-level offer with an undisclosed affiliate or subcontractor.
- **Quality/sustainability/risk.** Corporate scale is not a substitute for exact-site certification, change control or material declarations. **Disposition: first-wave Korean comparator only if the offer identifies the Bookuk factory, complete BOM and exact platform.**

### Why a public “cheapest” table would be false precision

The target is not an undecorated tube ex-factory. It is an accepted, filled, saleable Product Variant received at the named Southern California point. Each offer must be rebuilt through P3 using the [landed-cost model](./packaging-landed-cost-evidence-model.md):

```text
P3 cash cost per saleable unit =
  complete primary BOM cash
  + decoration, tooling, proofs and samples
  + secondary/tertiary packout
  + incoming inspection and quality loss
  + inbound freight, insurance, broker, duty/tariff and fees
  + filling, assembly, changeover, release and normal validated loss
  + finished-goods freight and receiving
  + MOQ surplus cash and first-run tooling cash
  divided by accepted saleable units
```

Report first-run cash and consumed economic cost separately. A 132-piece stock carton can beat a low unit quote by avoiding surplus and tooling; a domestic tube can beat a lower Asian ex-works price through freight, duty, lead-time and working-capital effects; an imported platform can still win if its component/decorating efficiency overwhelms logistics. These are hypotheses until E3 offers use the same specification and quantities.

For each supplier, request 1,000, 2,000 and 3,000 saleable-unit scenarios at Mini-light, trial-forward and balanced Mini/Full mixes. Record body, cap, seal and decoration MOQs independently. Do not assume one white body color pools across diameters or one print setup pools across SKUs.

## Quality, compatibility and recovery qualification

### Hard gates before weighted scoring

A candidate must pass the [supplier gates](./packaging-quality-compatibility-supplier-gates.md) before cost, sustainability or aesthetics can compensate:

1. **Identity gate:** named legal manufacturer, exact factory/site, disclosed subcontractors, tooling location and ownership.
2. **Specification gate:** signed drawing/revision and complete BOM with resin grades, layer order/thickness, colorants, coatings, inks, adhesives, cap, liner/seal and component masses.
3. **Quality-system gate:** site-level current certificates, control plan, traceability, retained samples, change notification, nonconformance/replacement terms and production-equivalent approval samples.
4. **Formula gate:** mass loss, appearance, odor, viscosity/pH, ingredient/pack interaction, stress cracking, swelling, paneling, seal/leakage, decoration adhesion and functional dispensing over accelerated/real-time conditions.
5. **Transport gate:** conditioned parcel and case testing in the chosen secondary/tertiary system, followed by leak, scuff, cap, recovery and appearance inspection.
6. **Regulatory gate:** declarations for all component materials and restricted substances; U.S. labeling space and California reporting inputs.

### Neutral shape-recovery protocol

Run the same protocol on every production-tool candidate with the actual Formula, or a documented worst-case simulant only until Formula is available:

1. Condition filled packs for at least 24 hours at the defined laboratory condition; record initial mass, dimensions and four orthogonal profile photographs.
2. Dispense a fixed mass through the proposed orifice using a defined hand/fixture compression location, travel and speed. Record peak/mean force and any stringing, suck-back, burping or cap contamination.
3. Photograph and measure profile immediately, at one minute and at 24 hours. Calculate residual deformation at defined body stations relative to the initial profile.
4. Repeat at nominal 0%, 25%, 50%, 75% and 95% dispensed life, with a fixed number of cycles and cap open/close events. Record spontaneous air return, paneling, seam marking and shoulder distortion.
5. At end of ordinary consumer evacuation, weigh recoverable residual and repeat using the supplier's recommended evacuation method without destructive cutting. Report residual as grams and percentage of filled mass.
6. Repeat key measurements after compatibility aging and transport simulation. A tube that rebounds when new but stiffens, swells, stress-cracks or leaks later fails.
7. Predefine pass bands for recovery percentage/time, force, residual, leakage and appearance. Keep raw images and measurements linked to sample lot, tool, BOM revision and fill lot.

Do not accept hand-squeezed videos, empty samples, different gauges, unprinted development pieces, or supplier-selected “similar” references as production proof.

## Sustainability and compliance screen

Sustainability remains a **15% weighted objective after hard gates**, using the [sustainability scoring method](./packaging-sustainability-scoring.md). Unsupported assertions score zero rather than receiving qualitative credit.

For each complete tube system—body, shoulder, cap, label/ink/foil, liner or membrane—collect:

- measured component mass and material/resin grade by part and layer;
- mechanically recycled and chemically recycled content separately, with chain-of-custody or mass-balance evidence, allocation and site/certificate scope;
- renewable/bio-based content with feedstock and accounting method;
- manufacturing site, energy/boundary claims and transport packout;
- APR/RecyClass or other design-assessment evidence for the **exact** structure, color, barrier, label, closure, ink and size, not a family marketing statement;
- actual U.S./California collection, sortation and reprocessing evidence before making a consumer recyclability claim;
- toxics-in-packaging and restricted-substance declarations for every part, ink, adhesive, coating and colorant;
- California SB 54 material/category and weight inputs plus change control.

Mono-PP or all-PE design can be advantageous, but it is not automatically curbside recyclable. EVOH percentage, tie layers, dark or carbon-black closures, metallic decoration, full-body labels, residual Product and local acceptance can change the outcome. Likewise, a PCR percentage is not meaningful unless the basis—tube body only or complete component, weight or mass balance, pre- or post-consumer—is explicit.

The [U.S./California constraints report](./us-california-cosmetic-packaging-constraints.md) governs dual-unit net-quantity space, identity/ingredient/business labeling, tamper and claims review, toxics documentation and SB 54 data. Avoid generic “FDA approved packaging,” “eco-friendly,” “100% recyclable,” or “non-toxic” statements unless the exact claim and evidence have been legally and technically reviewed.

## RFQ scoring and decision sequence

After #263 authorizes and standardizes outreach:

1. **Issue the same brief and drawing envelope** to the first-wave suppliers. Permit clearly labeled supplier alternatives, but do not let each supplier redefine the requirement.
2. **Screen identity, MOQ and exact size first.** Eliminate unnamed factories and unaffordable surplus before requesting extensive custom development.
3. **Obtain production-tool samples and declarations.** Test 30/120 and, where financially material, 40/100/150 alternatives in the same protocol.
4. **Run hard gates.** Only passing candidates reach commercial scoring.
5. **Normalize E3 offers to P3.** Apply the approved weights: landed cost 45%, technical/performance risk 20%, sustainability 15%, supplier reliability/lead time 10%, aesthetic fidelity 10%.
6. **Select finalists, then obtain E4 production offers** tied to approved drawings/golden samples, Formula/fill route, test responsibilities, schedule and quality remedies.
