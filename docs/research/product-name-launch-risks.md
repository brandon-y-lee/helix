# Product Name Launch Risks

**Research date:** 2026-08-10<br>
**Decision served:** preserve the final, user-locked Product Display Names and the launch gates that remain after the broader naming exploration was retired.

## Authority and limits

The six spellings below are the approved naming slate and supersede earlier alternatives. The underlying screen covered exact phrases, compressed forms, related word roots, the official [USPTO Trademark Search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/), and current beauty commerce. It was a bounded preliminary screen, not legal clearance.

Several names are descriptive or crowded. Use them with the **MEI PELLE** house mark and do not assume exclusive rights in the standalone wording. Trademark counsel must clear the complete composite before packaging or launch.

## Name-specific controls

### Balancing Prep

No clear direct blocker surfaced, but `balancing prep` is already ordinary commercial language and a dead Class 3 skincare application covered `BALANCE PREP STEP`. Keep claims to balanced-looking shine, refreshed feel, and preparation for the next routine step when substantiated. Do not claim biological pH change or normalized sebum. [USPTO serial 78670199](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/search/search-results/78670199)

### Polishing Prep

No live exact skincare record surfaced, but two dead Class 3 registrations used `INSTANT POLISHING PREP` for hair products. Use `polishing` only for the appearance of smoother, more refined surface texture. Do not imply abrasion unless the pad construction supports it, and do not publish strength language until Leaders provides exact acid concentrations, finished pH, stability, and tolerance data. [USPTO serial 85514692](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/search/search-results/85514692) · [USPTO serial 78695237](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/search/search-results/78695237)

### Beaming Prep

No exact or closely related Product-title hit surfaced in the bounded screen, but `Beaming` still communicates a result. Limit claims to brighter, more even-looking tone and the appearance of post-blemish marks. Do not claim acne treatment, scar removal, pigment suppression, bleaching, or guaranteed radiance.

### Chilling Prep

No exact beauty Product-title hit surfaced, but `Chilling` is a sensory promise. The TECA source formula contains Centella-derived materials, peppermint extract, and alcohol. Require finished-product tolerance and sensory testing plus discontinue-if-irritated guidance. Do not promise inflammation treatment, redness treatment, or suitability for every sensitive skin.

### Peptide Eye Cream

The exact phrase is common Product/category wording. A live longer Class 3 registration contains the full phrase, and Rhode's pending `PEPTIDE EYE PREP` occupies adjacent peptide eye-care language. The source formula declares several peptides, but Leaders must verify any PDRN percentage. Limit claims to hydrated, smoother-looking, brighter-looking, or more rested-looking eye contours when substantiated; do not claim bag removal or structural lifting. [USPTO serial 90905158](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/search/search-results/90905158) · [Rhode Peptide Eye Prep](https://www.rhodeskin.com/products/peptide-eye-prep-r-icon) · [USPTO serial 99309057](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/search/search-results/99309057)

### Peptide Nourish Mask

Meaning-adjacent nourishing-peptide mask names exist in current skincare, but the hard blocker is formula truth: Leaders' current LIFT declaration lists Sodium DNA and hydrolyzed collagen but no peptide. Do not publish this name in packaging, PDP copy, metadata, or campaigns until a signed production specification and U.S. INCI identify the peptide ingredients and support the associated claims. [Leaders PDRN 0.5% Lifting Mask](https://www.leaderscosmeticsusa.com/products/leaders-pdrn-0-5-lifting-mask)

## Launch gates

1. Clear every complete **MEI PELLE + Product Display Name** composite across federal, state, common-law, marketplace, domain, handle, owner-family, and intended international markets.
2. Preserve the six approved spellings exactly; do not silently restore earlier alternatives.
3. Reconcile every cosmetic claim with the exact OEM formula ID, signed INCI, label, directions, warnings, and finished-product substantiation.
4. Keep **Peptide Nourish Mask** blocked until the production formula truthfully supports `Peptide`.
5. Treat this record as preliminary risk context, not a legal opinion or final clearance.
