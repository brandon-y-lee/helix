# Leaders Formula and Catalog Evidence

**Research date:** 2026-08-09<br>
**Decision served:** establish the factual supplier-formula and current-Catalog baseline for Mei Pelle's catalog strategy. This note does not choose final names or a final portfolio.

## Scope and source handling

This review used three source classes:

1. a read-only snapshot of Mei Pelle's canonical Catalog;
2. every page of a private, 51-page Leaders supplier deck, reviewed as both rendered pages and OCR; and
3. current official Leaders USA, Leaders Korea, and FDA sources.

The supplier deck is paraphrased only where needed for the decision; page references identify supplier assertions without republishing confidential pages. Online status was checked on 2026-08-09. Retail availability can change and does not establish OEM production availability.

### Evidence and status legend

- **V — verified fact:** observed in the canonical Catalog, a current official product page, its live storefront availability state, or an FDA source.
- **S — supplier assertion:** a benefit, sensory statement, use, or classification asserted by Leaders; it is not independent clinical substantiation.
- **I — inference:** a reasoned Catalog implication that still needs commercial, regulatory, or formulation validation.
- **U — unknown:** not established by the sources reviewed.
- **Current — US/KR:** an exact Product or formula-family listing was live and orderable on the official US or Korean storefront. A different retail pack size is called out.
- **Unfindable:** the exact deck SKU was absent from both current official catalogs after exact-name review. This does not mean Leaders cannot manufacture it.
- **Sold out:** reserved for a live official unavailable state. No exact deck SKU was conclusively live-and-sold-out at the check.
- **Confidence H/M/L:** high = exact current Product plus usable ingredient evidence; medium = current formula family but a size, naming, or INCI gap; low = exact SKU unfindable or evidence is deck-only.

“Official INCI: yes” means an official retail page publishes an ingredient declaration. It is not a substitute for the signed Complete INCI and manufacturing specification for Mei Pelle's exact OEM Formula.

## Decision summary

- **V:** The canonical Catalog contains six Active Products, all internally marked available with an in-stock variant: CLEANSE, TREAT, SEAL, REFINE, FRAME, and LIFT. The Core is CLEANSE + TREAT + SEAL. There is no Active PROTECT Product.
- **I:** The current CLEANSE and TREAT sources remain the strongest evidence-backed Core candidates: Calming Biotics Gel Cleanser is the most broadly usable cleanser in the deck, and PDRN 5% Active Ampoule is the clearest signature active with a current Complete INCI, disclosed Sodium DNA concentration, and a low-friction daily format.
- **V:** Green Collagen Hydrate Boosting Cream is both deck-backed and currently available, but its official Korean declaration contains bergamot, rosemary, spearmint, litsea, and clary oils. That conflicts with the preferred fragrance/essential-oil-free Core posture and makes the current SEAL a deliberate tradeoff, not a neutral default.
- **I:** Calming Biotics Intensive Cream is the strongest deck-backed SEAL replacement to investigate because its current declaration has no obvious fragrance or essential oil. It is not yet cleared: Leaders' barrier-lipid marketing and the published ingredient declaration appear inconsistent because the declaration names Ceramide AP but not cholesterol.
- **V:** The current REFINE source, Pore Tightening Pad, has no AHA, BHA, or PHA in its published Complete INCI. Leaders presents it as a daily toner/pore-elasticity pad. Current Mei Pelle “active,” “controlled,” and gradual-cadence framing is therefore unsupported unless a different OEM Formula is intended.
- **I:** Comfort Barrier Sun Cream is the most plausible deck PROTECT lead for a US launch because its apparent UV active is zinc oxide. It is not launch-ready evidence: zinc-oxide percentage, US OTC testing, Drug Facts, broad-spectrum/SPF substantiation, manufacturing registration, and exact OEM Formula all remain unknown.
- **V:** Catalog and supplier evidence needs reconciliation before portfolio decisions: two Core Products lack Complete INCI internally; several deck/retail sizes conflict; TREAT numbering conflicts with its slug; one Green Collagen deck title is reused for two different items; and multiple legacy deck SKUs are no longer findable in their exact size.

## Bottom line

The evidence supports retaining Calming Biotics Gel Cleanser and PDRN 5% Active Ampoule as the factual CLEANSE/TREAT baseline. The Core decision now turns on SEAL: keep the well-evidenced but essential-oil-containing Green Collagen cream, or validate Calming Biotics Intensive Cream as the lower-conflict replacement. PROTECT should be incorporated into the catalog plan, but no candidate is cleared for a US launch from the evidence currently available. The supplier questions above are the minimum gates between a promising retail source and a defensible Mei Pelle Product.
