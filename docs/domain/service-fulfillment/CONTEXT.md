# Service & Fulfillment

Service & Fulfillment defines how Mei Pelle assists Customers, fulfills Paid Orders, handles post-purchase outcomes, and communicates service availability.

## Language

**Support Inquiry**:
A Visitor or Customer request for information or assistance from Mei Pelle.
_Avoid_: Support Case, Private Feedback

**Inquiry Type**:
The canonical subject classification assigned to a Support Inquiry for preparation or routing.
_Avoid_: Support Category, Support Case

**Support Intake**:
The process through which Mei Pelle receives and routes Support Inquiries through a Support Channel. Support Intake is not currently a Current Capability.
_Avoid_: Contact Page, Support Case

**Support Channel**:
A verified public destination through which a Customer can submit a Support Inquiry. Mei Pelle does not currently publish a Support Channel.
_Avoid_: Contact Page, Social Channel

**Service Status**:
A factual statement of whether a Mei Pelle service is a Current Capability, a Planned Capability, limited, or unavailable.
_Avoid_: Marketing Claim, Provider Status

**Fulfillment**:
The post-payment process that prepares and delivers the goods fixed in a Paid Order. Fulfillment is not currently a Current Capability.
_Avoid_: Checkout, Shipment

**Shipment**:
The physical group of Order Lines dispatched together during Fulfillment.
_Avoid_: Order, Fulfillment, Delivery

**Tracking**:
The Customer-visible information describing a Shipment's movement and carrier status.
_Avoid_: Shipment, Delivery

**Delivery**:
The transfer of a Shipment to its destination or authorized recipient.
_Avoid_: Shipment, Fulfillment

**Return**:
The Customer's transfer of eligible delivered goods back to Mei Pelle under an applicable Policy.
_Avoid_: Refund, Exchange, Cancellation

**Return Window**:
The period after Delivery in which an eligible Return may be initiated.
_Avoid_: Refund Period, Processing Time

**Item Claim**:
A Customer report that an Order Line was delivered damaged, missing, or incorrect.
_Avoid_: Return, Support Inquiry

**Replacement**:
Goods provided as an approved remedy for an Item Claim without treating the remedy as an Exchange.
_Avoid_: Exchange, Refund

**Exchange**:
A post-delivery arrangement replacing returned goods with a different Product Variant or Product.
_Avoid_: Replacement, Return, Refund
