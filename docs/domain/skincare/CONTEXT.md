# Skincare

Skincare defines The System, the composition of a Customer-appropriate Routine, and the ingredient and formulation language used to understand skincare.

## Language

**Ingredient**:
A substance included in a Product Formula and represented through its ingredient declaration.
_Avoid_: Key Ingredient, Formula

**Formula**:
The complete composition and form of a Product as made from its Ingredients.
_Avoid_: Product, Complete INCI

**Ingredient Index**:
Customer education that explains Ingredients used across helix Products without replacing Product-specific Complete INCI.
_Avoid_: Complete INCI, Ingredient Declaration

**The System**:
The fixed seven-role helix skincare architecture: CLEANSE, REFINE, TREAT, FRAME, SEAL, PROTECT, and LIFT.
_Avoid_: Method, Protocol, Routine

**System Step**:
A functional skincare role within The System. A System Step exists independently of any Product and may be fulfilled by no Product or multiple Products.
_Avoid_: Product, Routine Step

**System Position**:
The fixed ordinal assigned to a System Step, from 01 CLEANSE through 07 LIFT.
_Avoid_: Step Number, Product Number

**System Step Name**:
The canonical uppercase name of a System Step: CLEANSE, REFINE, TREAT, FRAME, SEAL, PROTECT, or LIFT. A bare System Step Name refers to the Step; use a complete Product name or Product Number when referring to a Product.
_Avoid_: Product Name

**Routine**:
A Customer-appropriate selection and schedule drawn from The System.
_Avoid_: System, Method

**Routine Position**:
The ordinal occupied by a System Step within a particular Routine, independent of its fixed System Position.
_Avoid_: Step Number, System Position

**Routine Schedule**:
The timing structure that assigns parts of a Routine to recurring use periods.
_Avoid_: Routine Group, Routine Position

**Routine Timing**:
A recurring use period within a Routine Schedule: morning, evening, or weekly.
_Avoid_: Routine Group, Usage Time, Product Use Cadence

**Product Use Cadence**:
The recurring periods or frequency at which a Product is intended to be used, which may relate it to one or more Routine Timings or to an as-needed schedule.
_Avoid_: Usage Time, Routine Timing, Usage Guidance

**Skin Concern**:
A cosmetic condition or goal a Customer may use when considering Product suitability, without representing a medical diagnosis.
_Avoid_: Skin Type, Medical Condition

**Skin Type**:
A broad pattern of skin characteristics used to inform Product suitability, without guaranteeing that one Formula is appropriate for every Customer with that pattern.
_Avoid_: Skin Concern, Medical Diagnosis

**Texture**:
The sensory form and feel of a Formula during application.
_Avoid_: Finish, Product Type

**Finish**:
The sensory or visible result a Formula leaves after application.
_Avoid_: Texture, Product Benefit

**Usage Guidance**:
Product-specific direction about application, amount, sequence, frequency, or Routine Timing.
_Avoid_: Medical Advice, Product Claim, Product Use Cadence

**Caution**:
A Product-specific limitation or safety instruction that should be considered before or during use.
_Avoid_: Usage Guidance, Medical Advice

**Complete INCI**:
A Product's complete ingredient declaration.
_Avoid_: Ingredient Highlights, Key Ingredients

**Key Ingredient**:
An ingredient selected for Customer education, not a substitute for Complete INCI.
_Avoid_: Complete INCI, Full Ingredients

**Formula Note**:
Explanatory context about a Product's formulation, not a substitute for Complete INCI or an ingredient declaration.
_Avoid_: Complete INCI, Ingredient Declaration
